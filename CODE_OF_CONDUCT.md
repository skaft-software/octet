# Code of conduct

## Welcome and standards

octet welcomes everyone, regardless of identity, background or experience.
Treat people with respect, give constructive feedback and take responsibility
for mistakes. Strong technical disagreement is welcome: challenge ideas and
evidence, not people's worth or right to participate.

Personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, harassment, threats, unwanted sexual
attention and sharing someone's private information without permission are not
acceptable. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in
its review is not acceptable. The same standards apply to maintainers.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces, project-affiliated channels and when
people represent octet in public, including at events or through official
accounts.

## How decisions are made

octet is maintainer-led.
Decisions follow technical evidence and project goals, not votes.
Anyone may propose, question or raise a release blocker with evidence.
Nobody has a veto.
An issue or pull request does not guarantee acceptance.

## Reporting

Report privately to [contact@skaft.org](mailto:contact@skaft.org). Include what happened
and any relevant context. Reports about the maintainer can also go to GitHub
via [Report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, respect reporters' privacy
and share details only as needed to handle the report. Security vulnerabilities
go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this conduct-reporting path.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation, and explain
moderation decisions when appropriate. Consequences follow the Contributor
Covenant's ladder, considering the severity and pattern of behavior:

1. **Correction:** a private written explanation of the violation and what must
   change; an apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for repetition and a specified period
   of no contact with the people involved, including those enforcing this code.
3. **Temporary ban:** a specified period without community participation or
   public or private contact with the people involved.
4. **Permanent ban:** permanent exclusion from community participation for
   sustained or severe violations.

Serious violations may lead directly to a temporary or permanent ban; earlier
steps are not prerequisites. Violating restrictions may lead to escalation.
