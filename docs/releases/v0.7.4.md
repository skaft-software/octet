# octet 0.7.4

Released on **2026-09-11** from commit
`fdb44e65d1f4d2bb5b5f95b54c7ad8b92bc11b34`.
The [GitHub release](https://github.com/skaft-software/octet/releases/tag/v0.7.4)
contains 28 assets: signed native binaries and installer, matching Serve
packages, the four official executable bundles, checksums, signatures and metadata.

## Highlights

- Fix the three medium-severity Vitest dependency alerts, alongside the scoped
  maintenance changes merged in #411.
- Overlap independent custom-provider discovery in batches of at most four,
  preserving registry order, validation and caches.
- Avoid whole-branch reference allocation when reconciling recent provider usage,
  and immediately skip empty ledgers. Full context estimation and ledger lookup
  are not constant-time. [Matched local measurements](../benchmarks/v0.7.4-performance.md)
  retain their source scope; they are not a final-build or live-provider speedup claim.
- Replace qualified interrupted Codex inference within bounded recovery without
  restarting tasks or replaying committed tools. Pre-send network waiting is
  independently paced and cancellable; auxiliary operations retain their own
  recovery status and host-owned controls.
- Persist failed-attempt usage uncertainty across resume and branch checkout.
  Show known subtotals separately from complete totals, and reject resumed hard
  cumulative ceilings when unresolved exposure makes enforcement impossible.
- Keep held-API status responsive, including length-adaptive activity shimmer.
- Improve ANSI256 Light added-diff contrast and confirmation/outcome geometry.
  ANSI256 and ANSI16 splash logos use a uniform model accent; true-colour
  terminals retain their gradient and no-colour terminals retain their foreground.
- Align product, SDK, Serve, installer/image and four official bundle distribution
  versions to 0.7.4. Bundles require exactly octet 0.7.4. Independent extension
  APIs 0.1/0.2/0.3, native-host protocol 1 and schema versions are unchanged.

## Verification and availability

- Protected [PR #413](https://github.com/skaft-software/octet/pull/413) merged
  after [candidate CI](https://github.com/skaft-software/octet/actions/runs/34622604875)
  passed; [main CI](https://github.com/skaft-software/octet/actions/runs/34623542363)
  also passed. The tested candidate and canonical merge have identical source trees.
- The [native workflow](https://github.com/skaft-software/octet/actions/runs/34624564404)
  passed builds, checksum/Sigstore verification and public installation on macOS
  Apple silicon, macOS Intel and GNU/Linux x86-64.
- The [Serve/bundle workflow](https://github.com/skaft-software/octet/actions/runs/34624564617)
  passed signed packaging and official installation/update checks.
- Hosted archive digests matched both published checksum manifests.

npm, Homebrew, crates.io and SDK registries were not published by this release.
Serve remains experimental. Installing extensions does not enable them or
persist trust grants. Website deployment is separate from native publication.

## Known limitations and documentation correction

The maintainer reported Apple Terminal, Ghostty and Ghostty-to-Ubuntu SSH
acceptance before publication. **A subsequent Ghostty-to-SSH model-switch report
shows stale splash rows and logo colours.** The targeted fix is described in
[0.7.5](v0.7.5.md); earlier acceptance is not proof that this journey worked.

The documentation bundled in the immutable 0.7.4 assets incorrectly retained
pre-release wording saying 0.7.4 was unpublished and #382 was unrun. That wording
was stale, not the release state. This corrected record does not replace the
0.7.4 tag or signed assets.

The [recovery qualification record](../qualification/v0.7.4-recovery.md) separates
local deterministic and PTY evidence from maintainer observations. Weeks-long
endurance, controlled repeated live faults and full Codex parity are not claimed.
